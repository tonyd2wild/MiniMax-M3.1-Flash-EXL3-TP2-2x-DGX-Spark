### What the numbers say

- **Peak aggregate is at C4** for every type (65-78 tok/s; 48 on prose).
- **Prefill is the weak spot:** ~500-570 tok/s, so a 128K-token prompt is ~4 minutes to first token.
- **There is a cliff at C5:** per-stream speed halves (code 17.4 -> 9.9 tok/s) and aggregate drops ~30%. With 3 draft
  tokens each stream verifies 4 tokens per step, so C5 is the first level past 16 rows per step; most likely the
  EXL3 expert kernels switch to a slower path there (vcruz305 tuned for one stream). To confirm after the run.
- **Prose is half the speed of code/math** because DSpark acceptance on prose is ~33% (2 tokens per step vs 3-4).
